FAQ

What to send before a quote.

How fast can a small job start?

Most fixed-scope jobs start after one sanitized sample, the expected output, and the failing command or current workflow are clear.

Can you work on private repos?

Yes, if access is authorized and the work can be verified without posting secrets or production customer data in public.

Can I pay for diagnosis first?

Yes. A narrow audit can identify the next patch, missing tests, or automation plan before a larger implementation quote.

What makes a request easy to quote?

A fake-data sample, the expected output, a failing command, and a clear done condition usually make the first quote faster and safer.

What does the handoff include?

A script, patch, review, or prototype with a short README, verification commands, and any known test or environment gaps.

What should not be shared?

Do not send passwords, API keys, payment details, customer records, production dumps, or files you are not allowed to share.

How are payment details handled?

Payment and platform terms are handled outside the public brief. The public request should contain scope only.
